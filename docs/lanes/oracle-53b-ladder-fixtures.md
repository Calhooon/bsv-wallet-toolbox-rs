The work: a fixture per provider answer pattern for each ladder, replayed by `cargo test --test fixtures`.
- The harness: `tests/fixtures/ladders.rs`, loaded by `tests/fixtures/main.rs` with `mod ladders;` (a sibling lane writes `main.rs` with `mod storage;`; write your own `main.rs` with only `mod ladders;`, the captain merges the two lines). Each fixture in `tests/fixtures/data/ladders/<ladder>/<name>.json` names the ladder (`get_raw_tx`, `get_merkle_path`, `get_utxo_status`, `get_status_for_txids`, `is_utxo`, `is_utxo_with_spender`, `get_utxo_leads`, `get_header_for_height`, `get_chain_tip_header`), the configured providers in order, each provider's answers per route as a mockito script (status, headers such as `Retry-After`, body), the call's arguments, and `expected`: the verdict or result (normalized) and the request count per provider and route. The harness stands up mockito servers from the script, builds `Services` pointed at them, makes the call, asserts the verdict and the counts.
- The patterns, per ladder, per rung where the ladder distinguishes them: 200 clean; 404 (a gap against another's 200; two clean negatives); 429 with `Retry-After` and without (the RateGate closes the rung, no request on the next call within the window); 5xx; a timeout (mockito delay past the client's timeout, kept short); a malformed body; a body cut short. The ladder's rules under test are the ones `services.rs` documents: a fault is could-not-look, a negative needs two clean answers, a closed rung costs no request.
- The CI job: none of your own; the sibling lane adds `fixtures` to `ci.yml`. If it is absent when you finish, say so for the captain.
- The proof: the replay green; a planted rule change (say, one negative taken as the verdict) shows a diff and a red run, reverted; the tails in the report. Two runs on one tree give identical fixture files; the mockito ports never appear in a fixture.

Bounds: no change to `src/` beyond test-only helpers; none to the public API; no `Cargo.toml` edit (mockito and tokio are present). Eight hours; past them, the harness with what ladders exist, and the list that remains.
